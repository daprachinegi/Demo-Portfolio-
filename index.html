INVESTIGATIVE DIVISION: AI & Applied Machine Learning

INVESTIGATION SUMMARY

Modern high-dimensional datasets often mask adversarial perturbations that slip past conventional linear classification boundaries. SYNAPSE-NET formulates anomaly detection as a latent reconstruction problem. By compressing input vectors into a bottleneck manifold, non-conforming anomalies trigger elevated reconstruction delta scores.

FORENSIC ARCHITECTURE

• Input Layer: Normalized continuous feature vector.
• Encoder: Dense layers with LeakyReLU activations and dropout regularization.
• Bottleneck: Low-dimensional latent space capturing foundational manifold distribution.
• Decoder: Mirror architecture minimizing Mean Squared Error (MSE) loss.
• Anomaly Scoring: Mahalanobis distance metric combined with per-feature residual mapping.

4 YEARS OF MASTERY

Playing the flute for four continuous years teaches physical and mental micro-calibration. Because the flute lacks a reed, sound generation depends entirely on splitting the embouchure air stream across the embouchure hole.
